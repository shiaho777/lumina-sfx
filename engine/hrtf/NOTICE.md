# SADIE II headphone responses

Copyright 2018, University of York. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Cal Armstrong, Lewis Thresh and Gavin Kearney recorded and developed the SADIE II database at the University of York Audio Lab. The accompanying LICENSE.txt applies to the measurement data.

Lumina selected a 15-degree grid from the diffuse-field-equalized H3 human subject measurement. The 44.1 kHz source contains 256-tap filters and two ears. Each grid point uses the nearest measured direction on the unit sphere. The poles each use one response. The table starts at −90° elevation, increases in elevation and uses positive azimuth towards the listener’s left. The PCM16 values are packed as base64 for local TypeScript delivery.
